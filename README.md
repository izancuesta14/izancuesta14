# Hi, I'm Izan 👋

I'm an **Artificial Intelligence Engineering** student at the University of Alicante (Spain). I like understanding *why* a model works or fails, not just getting it to run: choosing the right metric, checking for data leakage, and looking at what the errors actually mean.

I'm looking for internships and junior opportunities in **machine learning, deep learning and data science**.

## What I work on

- **Machine learning:** supervised and unsupervised learning, imbalanced data, ensembles and boosting, clustering, anomaly detection
- **Deep learning:** neural networks, generative models (autoencoders) and training/regularisation trade-offs
- **Computer vision and medical imaging:** image analysis, feature extraction from MRI
- **Natural language processing:** text modelling and language-based AI systems
- **Reinforcement learning:** policy-gradient methods and variance reduction
- **Data engineering:** data cleaning, SQL, reproducible preprocessing pipelines
- **Responsible AI:** model evaluation beyond accuracy, explainability and fairness auditing
